# Group subscriptions

One buyer pays for 2–100 licensed reader seats through the creator's own Stripe account. FlockStack takes no application fee. Enable group purchases from Payments → Manage groups. The same implementation supports Cloud's connected Stripe accounts and the self-hosted owner's Stripe key.

Groups use existing regular monthly, quarterly or yearly tier prices per seat. Founding-cap, lifetime, pay-what-you-want, individual trial and individual offer modes are excluded. Closing purchases leaves existing groups and billing management available. Existing subscriptions keep their price; an unrecovered checkout whose price changed requires a new checkout identity. A best-effort technical guard limits new active groups and recent checkouts to 100 per newsletter; abandoned checkouts older than 24 hours do not consume that guard. Existing groups are never removed to meet it.

## Purchase and access

The public purchase page is `/reader/groups/buy?newsletter=<slug>` on this region's app address, independent of whether the publication's archive is open. The buyer confirms payment in Stripe Checkout. A stable UUID makes concurrent checkout retries recover one group/session. Different buyer, plan or quantity cannot reuse that identity; an unknown provider outcome older than 23 hours requires review. No buyer or reader becomes a subscriber by purchasing a group.

Verified payment webhooks reread the stored checkout and current Stripe subscription/customer/latest invoice. Group events dispatch separately from individual subscriber events. Active subscription status alone cannot extend seats: a paid invoice must match the subscription, licensed price, currency, quantity and period. An unpaid expansion retains only previously funded capacity and its original expiry. Current quantity reductions take effect immediately. Failed/canceled/unsupported billing grants no seats. Group access has no extra Stripe grace period. A provider failure does not invent a new paid period or financial amount.

The verified buyer receives a private 7-day management link. An explicit resend form gives the same generic acknowledgment for unknown addresses. The owner can rotate the management version to revoke old links. Managers can invite or revoke reader seats, refresh billing, read invoices and open the group billing portal. No management or invitation token is returned to an API/MCP agent. Pages are dynamic, private/no-store, no-referrer and noindex; analytics exclude reader routes.

An invitation reserves capacity but enrolls nobody. Only its recipient gets the signed acceptance link. Acceptance requires the invited email and an explicit subscription checkbox; possession of that emailed capability is the email proof. Existing suppression and deleted-contact rules remain. Like existing signups, accepting does not delete or reject records simply because the account exceeds its subscriber allowance; existing campaign plan limits still apply. Existing individual billing is never canceled. Email acceptance is recorded separately from seat activation, so a concurrent revocation cannot grant access. A signup explicitly consented to before a later seat conflict can remain subscribed without group access; retry or contact the creator to resolve the seat.

Capacity is decided by a revision check on one bounded group row. Invited and partially accepted seats reserve their positions. A quantity reduction preserves the first licensed positions; managers can revoke/reassign the others. Reissues invalidate older invitation links. Delivery is best effort through the existing transactional-email machinery, with rate caps and recorded provider acceptance; it is not an exactly-once email outbox. A concurrent current-provider read can require retrying an older click.

## Personal billing, invoices and data

Contacts retain an independent personal paid-access snapshot, gift/comp grant and bounded group references. A higher funded group tier can win while preserving the original sources. Revocation, expiry or payment failure recomputes fallback. Reader access and send-time paid checks reread the parent group. Audience/segment queries also select a valid personal or gift fallback when a group expires between jobs, in SQL without a full reader scan. Parent billing/revocation refreshes reader revisions even for a projection that already looks unchanged, invalidating earlier in-flight access writes. Groups do not import as individual subscribers, and held underlying/group tiers cannot be deleted; archive them instead.

Each checkout creates a separate group customer. The billing portal uses its own restrictive configuration: invoice history, card updates and cancellation at period end; no plan/quantity or buyer-email updates. It never changes the creator's default portal. If the creator adds unrelated subscriptions/invoices to that customer, the portal refuses it. That verification is bounded to 100 invoices and one subscription; larger or mixed history needs creator review in Stripe. Individual invoices remain readable through the subscription-filtered paginated invoice list.

Invoice values come from Stripe, in each currency's minor units; missing values stay null. Paid invoice status is not evidence of net cash or a payout. Observed subscription revenue counts the actual group price and quantity once, not every recipient as a separate subscription. The older individual list-price estimate is separate from actual group invoices.

Group records store buyer/recipient emails, provider references, current seat/consent dates, funded periods and operational versions. No card data is stored. Buyer identity is verified once; later Stripe customer email edits do not silently transfer management. Reader deletion clears matching seat identity and revokes links. Buyer financial records remain with the account while it exists. Account export includes `group-subscriptions.csv` and `reader-paid-sources.csv`, plus the existing financial exports; internal leases and link-version counters are omitted. Account purge includes groups. No group data is read across regions.

Migration `0040_group_subscriptions.sql` creates the group table/indexes. Apply it through the normal reviewed release process before deploying this source. It has not been applied by the local build. No Stripe configuration, live checkout, email or production migration was run during local verification. Live provider acceptance, actual delivery, deployment and human language review remain release checks.

Stripe references: [licensed quantities](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/quantities), [invoice listing](https://docs.stripe.com/api/invoices/list), [portal configurations](https://docs.stripe.com/api/customer_portal/configurations/create).
